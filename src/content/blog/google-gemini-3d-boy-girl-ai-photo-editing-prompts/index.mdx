## Step-by-Step Guide: How to Generate & Edit Photos in Gemini

Follow these 5 verified steps to achieve high photorealism and facial consistency in Google Gemini:

1. **Launch Google Gemini**: Open the [Google Gemini web app](https://gemini.google.com) or download the official Gemini mobile app on your Android / iPhone.
2. **Attach Your Reference Portrait**: Tap the **`+` icon** to upload a clear front-facing portrait of yourself with natural lighting.
3. **Copy Your Selected Prompt**: Tap the **Copy Prompt** button on any prompt card above.
4. **Customize Key Details**: In the prompt text, update the `YOUR NAME` or clothing colors to match your personal style while keeping the identity preservation tokens intact.
5. **Generate & Download**: Hit send. Gemini will produce high-resolution variations. Save your favorite and set it as your new Instagram or WhatsApp DP!
