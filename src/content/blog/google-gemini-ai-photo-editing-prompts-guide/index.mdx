## The 6-Part Anatomy of a Master-Tier Gemini Prompt

To generate photorealistic AI photos that look completely indistinguishable from real high-end camera shots, your prompts must follow our **6-Part Conditioning Framework**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│               AI-Q Master Prompt Conditioning Hierarchy                │
│                                                                        │
│  [1. Identity & Facial Conditioning Directive]                         │
│  [2. Scene-Anchor & Geometric Source of Truth]                         │
│  [3. Optical Framing & Focal Length (e.g. 85mm f/1.4 at eye-level)]   │
│  [4. Tactile Scene Facts & Tangible Handheld Props]                    │
│  [5. Dynamic Micro-Expression & Wardrobe Continuity]                   │
│  [6. Physical Volumetric Lighting & Natural Skin Subsurface Scattering] │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Identity Preservation Directive**: Instruct the model to maintain facial bone structure, skin undertones, eye color, and natural facial asymmetry from your uploaded reference image.
2. **Scene-Anchor Geometry**: Establish the fixed background architecture, ground plane, and environmental surfaces so the model doesn't hallucinate random floating elements.
3. **Photographic Lens Physics**: Specify real optical lenses (e.g., *Canon RF 85mm f/1.2L*, *Hasselblad 80mm*, *Kodak Portra 400*) to get genuine optical bokeh rather than artificial gaussian blur.
4. **Concrete Tangible Props**: Give your subject realistic items to interact with (e.g. *ceramic matte coffee cup*, *vintage leather steering wheel*, *squeeze water bottle*).
5. **Wardrobe Materiality**: Specify real textiles (e.g. *quarter-zipped heavy fleece*, *distressed lambskin leather*, *handloom Chanderi silk*).
6. **Physical Lighting & Texture**: Detail how light interacts with skin (subsurface scattering, authentic pore detail, sunlit flyaway hairs, no artificial skin smoothing).
