## How Gemini AI Image Generation & Editing Works

Google Gemini uses advanced multimodal vision models alongside image generators (like Imagen 3) to interpret rich photographic cues. To get ultra-realistic results without the "plastic AI look", your prompts should always specify:

1. **Subject Details**: Age, ethnicity, pose, and natural facial expressions.
2. **Wardrobe & Styling**: Fabrics (silk, linen, distressed leather), colors, and cultural accents.
3. **Lighting & Atmosphere**: Golden hour, cinematic rim light, neon volumetric glow, or soft studio diffusion.
4. **Camera Optics**: 85mm portrait lens, f/1.4 aperture, deep depth-of-field, and 8k detail.

<Callout type="info">
  **Pro Tip for Gemini Users**: Avoid generic words like *"beautiful"* or *"HD"*. Instead, use photographic terminology like *"shallow depth of field"*, *"kodak portra color science"*, and *"volumetric soft shadows"*.
</Callout>

## Best Practices for Viral AI Photos in 2026

When publishing AI-generated images to Instagram or YouTube, follow these guidelines:

1. **Upscale Before Posting**: Even 8k prompts generate at 1024x1024. Use a free AI upscaler (like Real-ESRGAN or Magnific) to upscale to 4K resolution.
2. **Color Grade in Lightroom**: Apply a subtle S-curve in Lightroom or Snapseed to blend AI highlights into realistic shadows.
3. **Use Accurate Aspect Ratios**:
   - **Instagram Posts**: `4:5` (1080x1350)
   - **Reels & Shorts**: `9:16` (1080x1920)
   - **Twitter / Web Banner**: `16:9` (1920x1080)
